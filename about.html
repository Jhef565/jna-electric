ABOUT

Built by someone who works on the systems.

JNA Electrical Solutions is a C10 licensed electrical contractor based in Los Angeles.

OUR STORY

Started from real facility work, not just a license.

JNA Electrical Solutions was founded to fill a specific gap: facilities running automated equipment and access control systems, and struggling to find an electrical contractor who understood the systems as well as the wiring behind them.

That background comes from years spent maintaining complex electronic systems in the field — parking and access infrastructure, payment terminals, and the networks connecting them — before bringing that same troubleshooting approach to electrical contracting under a C10 license.

Today, J&A takes on industrial automation, gate and door access control, and the electrical infrastructure projects that come with them, for property managers, facilities teams, and general contractors across the LA area.
