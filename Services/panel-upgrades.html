Panel Upgrades

Upgrade your electrical panel for improved safety, capacity, and code compliance.

Is Your Panel Holding You Back?

An outdated or undersized electrical panel is one of the most common — and dangerous — issues in Los Angeles homes and businesses. If your panel is more than 25 years old, frequently trips breakers, or can't support modern appliances, it's time for an upgrade.

J&A Electrical Solutions performs safe, permitted panel upgrades with minimal disruption to your property.

What We Offer

  • 100A, 200A, and 400A service upgrades
  • Full panel replacement and relocation
  • Sub-panel installation
  • Breaker replacement and additions
  • LADWP and SCE service upgrades
