EV Charger Installation

Level 1 and Level 2 EV charger installation for homes and businesses in Los Angeles.

Power Up Your Electric Vehicle

Los Angeles is one of the fastest-growing EV markets in the country — and having a home or commercial charger is no longer a luxury, it's a necessity. J&A Electrical Solutions installs Level 1 and Level 2 EV chargers quickly and safely.

California offers rebates and incentives for EV charger installation through programs like the LADWP EV program and state Clean Vehicle Rebate Project.

What We Offer

  • Level 1 (120V): Uses standard outlet, adds ~4-5 miles per hour of charging — good for low-mileage drivers
  • Level 2 (240V): Dedicated circuit, adds ~25-30 miles per hour — the gold standard for home charging
  • Dedicated 240V circuit installation
  • Panel upgrade for EV charging capacity
  • Garage and outdoor charger mounting
  • Commercial fleet charging stations
  • Tesla Wall Connector and universal EVSE installation
