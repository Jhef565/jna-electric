WHAT WE DO

Two disciplines, one crew.

Most electricians treat controls and access systems as an afterthought. We built J&A around them — so the automation and the electrical work behind it come from the same team, on the same visit.

01 — AUTOMATION

Industrial & commercial automation

PLC and relay panel installs, motor controls, conveyor and equipment wiring, and troubleshooting for facilities that run on schedules, not guesswork.

02 — ACCESS CONTROL

Gate & door access systems

Keypad, card, and app-based access control for gates, garages, and building entries — plus intercoms, CCTV tie-ins, and low-voltage wiring.

03 — ELECTRICAL

Supporting electrical infrastructure

Panel upgrades, sub-panels, conduit runs, and power drops that get built alongside every automation or access job — no second contractor needed.

HOW A JOB RUNS

From first call to signed-off system.

STEP 01

Scope the job

Tell us what you're working with — new install, repair, or a system that's acting up. Most quotes start from a photo or a short call.

STEP 02

Site visit & quote

We walk the panel, gate, or equipment in person and send a written quote before anything is scheduled.

STEP 03

Install or repair

Work is scheduled around your operating hours where possible, with parts and permits handled ahead of time.

STEP 04

Test & walkthrough

We test the system live with you before we leave, and walk through how to operate and maintain it.
