HOW A JOB RUNS

From first call to finished work.

STEP 01

Tell us what's going on

Call, text, or send the quote form. A photo of your panel or the problem area is usually enough to get started.

STEP 02

Site visit & written quote

We look at the work in person and send a written quote before anything is scheduled. No surprise charges.

STEP 03

Install or repair

Permits and parts are handled ahead of time, and we schedule around your day. We protect your floors and clean up when done.

STEP 04

Test & walkthrough

We test everything with you before we leave and show you how it works, including labeling your panel.
