SERVICES

Automation, access control, and electrical work.

Every service below is performed by the same licensed crew. From the low-voltage control wiring down to the panel it runs from.

01

Industrial & Commercial Automation

PLC & relay panel installation

New panel builds and installs for production lines, pump stations, and equipment rooms — wired, labeled, and documented.

Motor controls & VFDs

Motor starter and variable frequency drive installation, replacement, and troubleshooting for pumps, fans, and conveyors.

Conveyor & equipment wiring

Field wiring for conveyors, packaging lines, and process equipment, tied back into existing control systems.

Controls troubleshooting

Fault-finding on panels and equipment that are down or misbehaving — most root causes are found on the first visit.

02

Gate & Door Access Control

Gate & garage access systems

Keypad, card reader, and app/telephone-entry systems for vehicle gates and parking structures, including motor and loop wiring.

Door access & intercom

Card and fob access for building entries, electric strikes and maglocks, and video intercom install and repair.

Access control repair & diagnostics

Tracing intermittent faults on existing systems — miswired connections and corrosion are the most common causes we find.

CCTV & low-voltage tie-ins

Camera runs and low-voltage wiring integrated with your access system, on the same visit as the controller work.

03

Electrical Infrastructure

Panel & sub-panel upgrades

Service and sub-panel upgrades sized for the automation or access load you're adding, not just what's there today.

Conduit runs & power drops

Conduit and power runs from panel to equipment, indoor or outdoor, sized and routed to code.

EV charger & smart integration rough-in

Rough-in and circuits for EV chargers and smart building systems, planned alongside your other electrical work.

General commercial electrical

Lighting, receptacles, and general electrical work for the facilities we already maintain systems in.
